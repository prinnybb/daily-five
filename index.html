How to play

Five tests. Two minutes.

Each test scores 0–100, for a total out of 500. Everyone gets the same five puzzles today.

  • 01
    Time Sense: stop the clock on the target. It disappears after one second.
  • 02
    Mental Math: answer as many sums as you can in 20 seconds.
  • 03
    Reflex: tap the moment it turns green. Three tries, averaged.
  • 04
    Pattern: remember which tiles were filled in.
  • 05
    Quick Count: guess how many dots you saw.

Leaving a test halfway scores it zero, so refreshing won't buy a retry. Play every day to build your streak.
