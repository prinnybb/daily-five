Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

The short version

Daily Five has no accounts and no database. Your scores, streaks, levels and badges are saved only in your own browser. We never receive them. The site shows ads from Google, and Google uses cookies to do that.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress in your browser's local storage so it's there when you come back. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. Doing so resets your scores and streak.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ad Settings.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you'll be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time.

Fonts

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser downloads them, Google receives your IP address. See Google's privacy policy.

Hosting

Like nearly every website, our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the time of the request) for security and to keep the site running.

Children

Daily Five is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
